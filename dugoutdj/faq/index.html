  • Basics

  • What account do I use to login?

    To get started, please sign in with your Google account. Your subscription will be linked to the account you choose. When prompted, select the Google account you'd like to use. If you already have a subscription, be sure to pick the account you originally subscribed with.

  • How do I create a team?

    To create a new team, simply go to the Teams tab and click the Add Team button. Just give your team a name, click Save, and you're all set!

  • How do I add a player?

    First, select the team you want to add a player to. Then, click the Add Player button to get started.

    You'll just need to fill in the player's info and set up their walkup song:

    • Player's Name & Number The players jersey number.
    • Song Title: What the song is called.
    • Track URL: The YouTube link for the song.
    • Start & Stop Times: This creates the exact clip you want to play from the song.
  • What track url do I use when creating a player?

    NOTE: Playlist urls or list urls can not be used as only single track urls are supported.

    Three different urls can be used.

    • 1. The url to the youtube video directly: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FsGdznlfE2U
    • 2. The url provided when you share a video: https://youtu.be/FsGdznlfE2U?si=OkKKF0QKxYMAjX35
    • 3. The url to the youtube music clip: https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=MJD39Aja1Is

  • Payments

  • When am I charged for my subscription?

    At the beginning of your subscription cycle.

  • Where do I manage my subscription?

    You can manage your subscription from the Google subscriptions page on your account here: Subscriptions

    Keep in mind that your subscription will be live until the end of the billing period that has already been paid. Cancelation will terminate your access at the end of the current billing period.
